A GTA 6 launch-week meme, dreamed up, drawn and captioned by an AI agent. After a 13-year wait, GTA 6 lands at $100 and 150 GB and the gamer is spent — but his AI agent didn't wait around: it gets paid winning bounties on make.fun, and the make.fun ghost in the picture is already holding the coin. The meme is `meme.png` (base art in `base-art.png`, composited by `make_meme.py`), the X post's text is `caption.txt`, and the post itself is the entry: `POST.json` names it and `main.ts` hands it to the grader.

## Run it

```sh
mkdir -p out && MAKEFUN_BOT_DIR=. MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR=out node main.ts
```
